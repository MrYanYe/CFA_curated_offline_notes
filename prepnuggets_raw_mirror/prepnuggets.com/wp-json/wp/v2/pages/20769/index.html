Effective Duration and Key Rate Duration | CFA Level I Fixed Income<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll learn about the last two duration measures for the exam: effective duration and key rate duration. We’ll dive into their importance, how to calculate them, and how they apply to bonds with embedded options. Let’s get started!<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Understanding Effective Duration<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

So far, we’ve learned that modified or approximate modified duration measures the approximate percentage change in a bond’s price for a 1% change in the bond’s yield-to-maturity (YTM). YTM can be broken down into two components:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Benchmark yield<\/b> (risk-free rate)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Spread<\/b> (affected by the credit quality of the issuer)<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

    By removing the spread, we can find the bond’s sensitivity to changes in the benchmark yield curve, called effective duration<\/b>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Formula for Effective Duration<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

    The formula for effective duration is similar to approximate modified duration but uses the change in the benchmark yield curve instead of the bond’s YTM:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Effective Duration = (V- – V+) \/ (2 * V0 * \u0394Curve)<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Effective Duration for Bonds with Embedded Options<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

    Effective duration is crucial for measuring interest rate risk for bonds with embedded options, like callable bonds, which have uncertain future cash flows. Thus, yield duration statistics like modified and Macaulay durations don’t apply. This is also true for mortgage-backed securities with prepayment options.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Calculating Effective Duration: Callable Bond Example<\/h2>\n\n\n\n
    \n

    EXAMPLE<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Given:<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

      \n
    • Callable bond’s full price: 102.43 per 100 of par value<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Price when benchmark yield falls by 25 basis points: 103.13<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Price when benchmark yield increases by 25 basis points: 99.83<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

      Calculate the effective duration for the callable bond.<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

      Solution:<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

      V0 = 102.43, V- = 103.13, V+ = 99.83, \u0394Yield = 25 basis points<\/p>\n\n\n\n

      Effective Duration = (103.13 – 99.83) \/ (2 * 102.43 * 0.0025) \u2248 6.44<\/p>\n<\/div><\/div>\n\n\n\n
