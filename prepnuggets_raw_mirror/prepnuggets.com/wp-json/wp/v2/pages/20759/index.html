Today, we’ll explore modified duration and its related measure \u2013 the money duration. These measures help us better understand a bond’s interest rate risk.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Modified Duration: Estimating Bond Price Changes<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Modified duration provides an approximate percentage change in a bond’s price for a 1% change in yield to maturity. It is calculated as the Macaulay duration<\/b> divided by one plus the bond’s yield to maturity.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

ModDur = MacDur \/ (1+YTM)<\/strong><\/p>\n\n\n\n
