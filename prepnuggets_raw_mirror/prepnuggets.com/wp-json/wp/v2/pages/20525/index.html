Understanding and Analyzing Cash Flow Statements | CFA Level I FSA<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this comprehensive guide, we will delve into the analysis of cash flow statements. Our focus will be on:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Understanding sources and uses of cash<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Calculating and analyzing free cash flow (FCF)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Exploring various cash flow ratios<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Performing common-size analysis on cash flow statements<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

    Sources and Uses of Cash<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

    As a company progresses through its life cycle, its cash flow patterns change. Let’s break down how cash flow behaves in different stages of a company’s growth:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    A. Early Growth Stage<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

    During the early stages, a firm may experience:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

      \n
    • Negative operating cash flow due to cash used for purchasing inventory and developing capabilities.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Negative investing cash flow as the company acquires long-term assets.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • External financing through issuing debt or equity securities to cover negative cash flows.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

      B. Sustainable Stage<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

      A sustainable company has the following cash flow characteristics:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

        \n
      • Positive operating cash flow that exceeds capital expenditures.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
      • Provides returns to debt and equity holders.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

        Key aspects to analyze in cash flow statements:<\/b><\/p>\n\n\n\n

          \n
        • Operating cash flow: Determine the major factors affecting operating cash flow and examine the quality of the firm’s earnings.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Investing cash flow: Analyze capital expenditures and their implications on the company’s growth prospects.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
        • Financing cash flow: Evaluate the firm’s financing activities to understand its reliance on debt or equity and its use of cash for debt repayment, stock repurchase, or dividend payments.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
