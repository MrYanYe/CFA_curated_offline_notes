Understanding Digital Assets and Ledger Technology | CFA Level I<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Digital assets and distributed ledger technology (DLT), including blockchain, are revolutionizing financial markets and investment strategies. This guide aims to demystify these concepts for CFA candidates, offering insights into their mechanisms, categories, and implications for investors.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Introduction to Distributed Ledger Technology<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

At the core of digital assets lies distributed ledger technology (DLT), with blockchain being its most renowned form. Unlike traditional centralized ledgers managed by institutions like banks, DLT operates on a network spread across multiple participants. Each participant holds an identical copy of the ledger, ensuring transparency, immutability, and real-time accessibility.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Blockchain: A Paradigm Shift<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

Blockchain organizes data into blocks, each securely linked to the previous one through cryptographic hashes. This structure, combined with consensus mechanisms like proof of work or proof of stake, safeguards the network against manipulation by requiring validation from the majority of participants before adding new blocks. The innovation extends beyond security; it eliminates the need for central authorities, enabling trustless transactions.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
