Understanding Fiscal Policy<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Fiscal policy refers to a government’s use of spending and taxation<\/strong> to meet macroeconomic goals. In contrast, we’ve learned that monetary policy is the responsibility of the central banks which uses tools like policy rate and reserve requirements to adjust the money supply.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
