Understanding Yield Spreads | CFA Level I Fixed Income<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we will explore various yield spread measures, which can be divided into two main categories: yield spreads over benchmark rates and yield spreads over the benchmark yield curve.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Yield-to-Maturity Components: Benchmark and Spread<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

To comprehend changes in bond prices and yields-to-maturity, it’s crucial to break down a bond’s yield-to-maturity into two components:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  1. Benchmark yield<\/b>: Often a government bond yield, this serves as the base rate.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  2. Spread<\/b>: The difference between the yield-to-maturity and the benchmark yield.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n

    This separation is important for distinguishing between macroeconomic and microeconomic factors affecting bond prices and yields-to-maturity.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
