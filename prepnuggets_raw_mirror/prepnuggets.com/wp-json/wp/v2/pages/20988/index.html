Market Regulation: Ensuring Stability and Fairness | CFA Level I Equity Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Key Problems in Financial Markets<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Before diving into market regulation, let’s discuss some of the main issues that could arise in financial markets:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  1. Fraud and theft:<\/b> Unsophisticated investors may be vulnerable to agents or investment managers who take advantage of their trust.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  2. Insider trading:<\/b> Market insiders with privileged information could have an unfair advantage, which may discourage other traders and reduce market liquidity.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  3. High cost of market information:<\/b> Expensive information access can hinder informational efficiency and discourage investment.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  4. Risk of defaults:<\/b> Market participants may fail to honor their obligations, leading to serious consequences for individuals and firms.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n
