Professional Conduct Program and Enforcement<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The CFA Institute Board of Governors is responsible for the Professional Conduct Program (PCP). The Disciplinary Review Committee (DRC), consisting of volunteer CFA Charterholders, enforces the Code and Standards. The CFA Institute Bylaws and Rules of Procedure for Professional Conduct provide the structure for enforcement.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
