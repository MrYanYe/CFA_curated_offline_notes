How Securitisation Works | CFA Level I Fixed Income<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Let’s dive into the fascinating world of asset-backed securities<\/b>! These fixed-income instruments are created through a process called securitisation<\/b>. In this lesson, we’ll explore the securitisation process and learn about the benefits it brings to economies and financial markets.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Securitisation Process and Benefits<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Securitisation is a process where assets are transferred from their original owners to a separate legal entity called a special purpose vehicle (SPV)<\/b>. The SPV then issues securities backed by these assets and sells them to investors. The cash flows generated by the assets are used to pay both interest and principal owed to the investors.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
