Understanding Private Equity | CFA Level I Alternative Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Private equity<\/b> refers to investments in privately owned companies or public companies with the intent to take them private. These investments are usually categorized by their investment strategy, which includes leveraged buyouts, venture capital, and development capital.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
