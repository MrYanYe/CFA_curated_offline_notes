Cash Flow Additivity<\/a><\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The Cash Flow Additivity principle is crucial in finance, highlighting how the present value of a security is the sum of the present values of all future cash flows, each discounted by the security’s required return. It underpins the valuation of financial instruments and market equilibrium, demonstrating how arbitrage opportunities are short-lived in efficient markets.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
