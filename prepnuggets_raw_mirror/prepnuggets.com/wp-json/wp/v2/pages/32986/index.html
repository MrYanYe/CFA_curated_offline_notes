Operating Profitability: Leverage’s Role<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Leverage, or the proportion of fixed costs within a company’s cost structure, plays a pivotal role in determining operating profitability. A higher degree of leverage indicates a greater reliance on fixed costs, such as rent and salaries, which remain constant regardless of production levels. We differentiate between fixed operating expenses and fixed financing costs, with the former including costs like depreciation and the latter comprising interest payments.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
