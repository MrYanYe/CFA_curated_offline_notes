Functions of the Financial System<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

First things first, let’s have a quick recap of the financial system’s primary functions:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  1. Help participants achieve their purposes in using the financial system.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  2. Determine the returns that equate the total supply of savings with the total demand for borrowing.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  3. Allocate capital to its most efficient uses.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n
