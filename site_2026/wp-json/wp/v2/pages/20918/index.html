Commodity Prices and Investments | CFA Level I Alternative Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Today, we’ll explore factors that influence commodity prices and the mechanisms of pricing commodity futures contracts.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Factors Influencing Commodity Prices<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Spot prices for commodities are affected by supply and demand. Let’s break it down:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Supply:<\/b> Influenced by production costs, storage costs, and existing inventories.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Demand:<\/b> Affected by the value of the commodity to end-users, global economic conditions, and cycles.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Speculators:<\/b> Both supply and demand are impacted by non-hedging investors or speculators.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

    Due to long lead times to alter production levels and external forces like weather, commodity prices can be volatile. Investors can potentially benefit from this volatility by analyzing inventory levels, production forecasts, government policy changes, and economic growth expectations.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
