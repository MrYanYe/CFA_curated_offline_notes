Understanding Swaps and Their Connection to Forwards<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Welcome back to our discussion on the pricing and valuation of swaps. Today, we’ll dive into the general principles of swap pricing and take a closer look at interest rate swaps.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Swaps<\/b> share some similarities with forwards<\/b>, such as: Typically requiring no payment by either party at initiation. Being custom instruments with non-standard contracts. Largely less regulated. Exposing both parties to counterparty risk.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
