Exploring Bond Yield Measures | CFA Level I Fixed Income<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we will look at various bond yield measures and learn how to calculate and interpret them for fixed-rate bonds. We will also understand the differences in yields based on coupon payment frequency and other factors that affect yield calculations.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Yield Measures for Fixed-Rate Bonds<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Yield-to-maturity (YTM)<\/b> is a common yield measure for bonds, but there are other yield measures that provide more insight into the returns from investing in a bond. Let’s explore some of these alternative yield measures.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Yield and Periodicity<\/h3>\n\n\n\n

For bonds with different coupon payment frequencies, the holding period yield and the yield-to-maturity can be the same. However, the effective yield increases with the periodicity.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Annual-pay bond:<\/b> Effective yield is 10% (same as YTM)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Semi-annual coupon bond:<\/b> Effective yield is 10.25%<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Quarterly coupon bond:<\/b> Effective yield is 10.38%<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
