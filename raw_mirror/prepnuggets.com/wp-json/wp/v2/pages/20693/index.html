In this lesson, we’ll provide a comprehensive understanding of government and government-related bond sectors. Let’s start with sovereign bonds<\/b>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Sovereign Bonds: The Basics<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Sovereign bonds are issued by national governments or their treasuries and are backed by the government’s taxing power. Most governments issue bonds in their own currency, which usually have high credit ratings and are considered essentially free of default risk, thanks to their ability to collect taxes and print the currency.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
