Understanding Alternative Investment Performance | CFA Level I<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Introduction to Alternative Investment Risks<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Alternative investments carry risks that markedly differ from those associated with traditional, unleveraged, long-only investments. These risks are primarily related to the timing of cash flows, the strategic use of leverage, valuation challenges, and complex fee structures that influence the performance and return calculations of such investments.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Decoding Cash Flow Timing in Funds<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Understanding the life cycle of private equity and real estate development funds is crucial due to its significant impact on investment returns. This cycle is characterized by three phases: capital commitment, capital deployment, and capital distribution, each affecting the fund\u2019s cash inflows and outflows differently.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Capital Commitment Phase:<\/b> Early funding requirements lead to capital calls with minimal inflows.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Capital Deployment Phase:<\/b> Major capital outflows for funding commitments with some potential early exits or interim income.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Capital Distribution Phase:<\/b> Realization of gains from successful exits or property sales, leading to significant cash inflows.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
