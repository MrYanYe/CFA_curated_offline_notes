Welcome to the second part of our introduction to fixed income! In this lesson, we’ll discuss the contents of a bond indenture.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Trust Deed and Bond Indenture<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The trust deed<\/b>, also known as the bond indenture<\/b>, is a legal contract that outlines the bond’s structure, issuer’s obligations, and bondholder’s rights. A trustee, typically a financial institution, is appointed to ensure the issuer complies with the indenture and to take action on behalf of bondholders when necessary.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
