Why Financial Statement Analysis?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Companies are required to publish regular financial reports, providing information on their performance, financial position, and changes in financial position. Analysts use these reports, along with other information, to form expectations about a company’s future profitability and cash flow. This process, known as financial statement analysis, helps in making investment, credit, and other economic decisions.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
