Present Value Models – Gordon Growth Model | CFA Level I Equity Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll explore the popular Gordon Growth Model<\/b> and its extension, the multi-stage dividend discount model<\/b>. Let’s dive right in and discover how these models help us estimate the price of a stock in the future.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Gordon Growth Model Basics<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The Gordon Growth Model simplifies the dividend discount model, assuming that the growth rate of dividends remains constant forever. This allows us to express every future dividend in terms of the current or most recent dividend paid, denoted as D0<\/b>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
