Financial Analysis Techniques: Analytical Process, Tools & Techniques | CFA Level I FSA<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

This is an important topic because one of the key capabilities of an analyst is the ability to compute ratios and make inferences from these ratios. In this first part, we will revise the financial statement analysis framework and introduce four tools and techniques that are commonly used in financial statement analysis. Let’s dive right in.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Financial Statement Analysis Framework<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The financial statement analysis framework consists of six formal steps:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  1. State the objective and context:<\/b> Determine the questions to be answered, the presentation format, and the resources and time available for the analysis.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  2. Gather the data:<\/b> Acquire the company’s financial statements and other relevant data on its industry and the economy.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  3. Process the data:<\/b> Make appropriate adjustments to the financial statements, calculate ratios, and prepare exhibits such as graphs and common-size balance sheets.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  4. Analyze and interpret the data:<\/b> Use the data to answer the questions stated in the first step and decide what conclusions or recommendations the information supports.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  5. Report the conclusions or recommendations:<\/b> Prepare a report and communicate it to the intended audience.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  6. Update the analysis:<\/b> Repeat these steps and update the conclusions and recommendations periodically.<\/li>\n<\/ol>\n\n\n\n
