Understanding Economic Indicators | CFA Level I Economics<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we will conclude our discussion on business cycles by exploring economic indicators. We will delve into the differences between leading, coincident, and lagging indicators, as well as how to utilize them effectively.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Types of Economic Indicators<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

There are three types of economic indicators:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Leading indicators:<\/b> Change direction before peaks or troughs in the business cycle, helping strategists and businesses anticipate cyclical turns.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Coincident indicators:<\/b> Change direction at roughly the same time as the peaks or troughs, confirming the economy’s current phase.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Lagging indicators:<\/b> Change direction after expansions or contractions have already begun, as seen in the unemployment rate.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
