Monopolistic Competition: Pricing and Production Decisions | CFA Level I Economics<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll explore the pricing and production decisions for firms operating under monopolistic competition.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Characteristics of Monopolistic Competition<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Monopolistic competition is marked by the following characteristics:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Large number of independent sellers:<\/b> Each firm has a relatively small market share, and no individual firm has significant pricing power.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Low barriers to entry:<\/b> Firms are free to enter and exit the market. If existing firms are earning economic profits, new firms can be expected to join the industry.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Product differentiation:<\/b> Firms produce slightly different products in terms of quality, features, and marketing, making them close substitutes for one another.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

    Under monopolistic competition, firms compete on price, quality and features, and marketing due to product differentiation. They can set their own price and output as they face downward-sloping demand curves.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
