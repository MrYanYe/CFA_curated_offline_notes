Substitution and Income Effects<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

We have learned in the first lesson that the law of demand states that when the own price of a good falls and all else are equal, the demand for the good increases. According to this law, the demand curve for any good is downward sloping. We can attribute this to two effects – the substitution effect<\/b>, and the income effect<\/b>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
