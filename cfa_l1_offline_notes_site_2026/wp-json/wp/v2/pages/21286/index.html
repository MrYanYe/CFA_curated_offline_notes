Factors Affecting Capital Structure Decisions | CFA Level I Corporate Issuers<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll explore the factors that influence capital structure decisions, such as debt ratings, business characteristics, external factors, agency costs, and asymmetric information costs.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Debt Ratings and Their Impact on Capital Structure<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

As we learned in Fixed Income, credit rating agencies assess the credit quality of issuers and their debt issues, assigning ratings based on default risk. Debt ratings<\/b> are crucial for managers because they directly affect the company’s cost of capital.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Investment-grade bonds: Rated BBB- or higher<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Non-investment grade bonds: Rated BB+ or lower, often called high-yield bonds or junk bonds<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Defaulted bonds: Rated D by Standard & Poor’s and Fitch, or C by Moody’s<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
