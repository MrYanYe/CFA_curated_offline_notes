Exploring Hedge Fund Strategies | CFA Level I Alternative Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Let’s dive into the fascinating world of hedge funds<\/b> and explore their strategies! In this lesson, we’ll discuss some common characteristics of hedge funds and learn about the different strategies that hedge fund managers employ.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Common Hedge Fund Characteristics<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Despite the wide variety of hedge funds, they typically share the following characteristics:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Aggressively managed portfolios<\/b> that leverage long and short positions and often use derivatives.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Higher risks<\/b> in pursuit of high returns due to less regulation compared to traditional investments.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Partnership structure<\/b> with investors as limited partners and the management firm as the general partner.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Less liquidity<\/b> than traditional investments due to lockup periods and redemption restrictions.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
