In this lesson, we will explore different types of business models and how they affect a company’s business and financial risks. We’ll learn the key features of business models, discuss some common types, and examine how digital technologies have brought about innovative business models.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

What is a Business Model?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

A business model is a company’s strategy for making money. It explains how the company aims to provide its products or services, attract customers, deliver to them, and make a profit. A business model is not a detailed financial plan but rather provides an overview of the value proposition, value chain, and profitability.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
