Understanding Types of Equity Indexes | CFA Level I Equity Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

1. Broad Equity Market Indexes<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Broad equity market indexes<\/b> represent an entire equity market, typically including stocks that make up over 90% of the selected market. For instance, the Wilshire 5000 Index<\/b>, which consists of more than 6,000 US stocks, is a good representation of the overall performance of the U.S. equity market.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

2. Multi-Market Indexes<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Multi-market indexes<\/b> comprise indexes from different countries, representing multiple stock markets. They can be based on geographic regions, economic development groups, or even the entire world. These indexes are crucial for investors with a global approach to equity investments.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
