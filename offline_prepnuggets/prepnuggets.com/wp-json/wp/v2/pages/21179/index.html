Principal-Agent Relationship<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Let’s begin by learning what a principal-agent relationship<\/b> is.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Also known as an agency relationship, a principal\u2013agent relationship is created when a principal hires an agent to perform a particular task or service. An agent is obliged to act in the best interests of the principal. Conflicts arise when an agent’s interests may not coincide exactly with those of the principal.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
