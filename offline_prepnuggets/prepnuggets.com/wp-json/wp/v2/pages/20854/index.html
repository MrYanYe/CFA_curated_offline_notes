Pricing and Valuation of Options | CFA Level I Derivatives<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll explore the pricing and valuation of options. We’ll start with a review of basic option concepts, discuss the concept of moneyness, and learn about intrinsic and time values. Finally, we’ll go through the six factors that affect option values.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Basics of Options and the Concept of Moneyness<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

First, let’s recall that a call option<\/b> gives the holder the right, but not the obligation, to buy an underlying asset at a given price, while a put option<\/b> gives the right to sell. Options can be either American-style<\/b> (exercisable any time up to and including expiration) or European-style<\/b> (exercisable only on the expiration date).<\/p>\n\n\n\n
