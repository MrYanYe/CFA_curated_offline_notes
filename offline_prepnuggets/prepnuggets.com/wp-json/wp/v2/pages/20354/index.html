This lesson is a prerequisite<\/strong> for the course. While you won’t be directly tested on its content in the exam, it’s assumed you’ve gained this knowledge or skill during your university studies. Imports<\/b> are goods and services that firms, individuals, and governments purchase from producers in other countries. Exports<\/b> are goods and services that firms, individuals, and governments from other countries purchase from domestic producers. Net exports<\/b> is the value of a country’s exports minus the value of its imports over some period.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

There is a trade surplus<\/b> when net exports are positive, and a trade deficit<\/b> when net exports are negative.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
