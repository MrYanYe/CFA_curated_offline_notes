Inventory Expense Recognition: An Introduction<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Expenses are subtracted from revenue to calculate net income. Under accrual accounting, revenue is recorded when earned, regardless of cash payment. But when should we record the expenses for a sale?<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Expense recognition<\/b> is based on the matching principle<\/b>, where expenses are recognized in the same period as the revenue they generate. Let’s examine this through the lens of inventory.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
