We now move on to an in-depth examination of the various financial statements, starting with the income statement<\/b>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

The income statement is a crucial piece for an analyst to make forecasts of the future earnings of a firm. As such, an analyst should know the key components of an income statement, how they are prepared, and the techniques to analyse the statements.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Components of the Income Statement<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

The income statement communicates how much revenue<\/b> a company generated during a period, and the expenses<\/b> it incurred in connection with generating that revenue. The key outcome from the income statement is the net income<\/b>, which is a key metric of the company\u2019s profitability.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
