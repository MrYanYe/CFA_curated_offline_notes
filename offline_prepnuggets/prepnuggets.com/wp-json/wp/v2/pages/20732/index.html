What is a Residential Mortgage Loan?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

A residential mortgage loan<\/b> is a loan where the underlying collateral is residential real estate. In case of a borrower’s default, the lender has a legal claim to the collateral property.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
