Intrinsic Value vs Market Price | CFA Level I Equity Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll explore the difference between the intrinsic value of a stock and its market price, as well as the challenges of using such an approach.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Intrinsic Value: The Fundamental Value of a Stock<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

In the topic on market efficiency, we learned that the intrinsic value<\/b> of a company stock is the value assigned by rational investors if they had a complete understanding of the stock’s characteristics. The intrinsic value is also known as the fundamental value<\/b> because it’s based on the firm’s fundamentals and characteristics. An analyst uses these inputs in their valuation model, along with their assumptions, to estimate the intrinsic value.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
