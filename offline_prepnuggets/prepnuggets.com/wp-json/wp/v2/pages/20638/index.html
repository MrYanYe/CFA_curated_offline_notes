Today, we’ll discuss the common adjustments analysts often make to increase the accuracy and comparability of a company’s financials.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Comparing Financials: A Reality Check<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

When comparing a company’s statements and ratios against its peers, we assume they use the same accounting standards and methods. However, this is often not the case. Companies can have different accounting methods, even under the same standard, and local accounting standards can differ across nations.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

It’s an analyst’s duty to adjust the financial statements of the firm under evaluation to make comparisons fair. Let’s look at some common adjustments.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
