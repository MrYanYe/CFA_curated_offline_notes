Exploring Types of Fixed Income Indexes | CFA Level I Equity Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’re diving into the different types of fixed income indexes.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Compared to equities, the fixed income universe is much broader. Fixed-income securities vary widely in these dimensions:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Geographical location of the issuer<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Type of issuer (government, government agency, corporate, structured finance entity)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Business sector for corporate issuers (energy, finance, healthcare, technology)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Credit quality (investment grade or non-investment grade)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Time to maturity (from a few days to 30 years or beyond)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Coupon structure and rates (zero-coupon, fixed coupon, floating rate notes)<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Embedded options (convertibility, callable, putable bonds)<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
