Mastering Working Capital and Liquidity Management | CFA Level I<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

This lesson delves into the strategic management of working capital and liquidity, crucial for ensuring a company’s operational efficiency and financial stability. Learn about different approaches to managing working capital and the principles of liquidity management that can maximize a company’s value while mitigating liquidity risk.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Understanding Working Capital Management<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Effective working capital management balances the need for liquidity with the goal of maximizing firm value. Key concepts include:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC):<\/b> Measures the time between cash expenditure for raw materials and receiving payment from customers.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Approaches to Working Capital:<\/b> Strategies vary from conservative to aggressive, impacting liquidity risk and financing cost.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
