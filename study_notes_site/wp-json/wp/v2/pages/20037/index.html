In this lesson, we’ll explore two more types of elasticities: the cross-price elasticity of demand and the income elasticity of demand.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Adding Variables to the Demand Function<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

In our previous lesson, we learned about own-price elasticity of demand, demand function, and elasticity coefficient. Now, we will add two more independent variables to the demand function: the price of related goods<\/strong> and consumers’ income<\/strong> levels<\/strong>. We can use their coefficients to calculate the cross-price elasticity of demand and income elasticity of demand.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
