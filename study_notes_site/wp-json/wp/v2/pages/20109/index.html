Components of GDP and Economic Sectors<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

In our last lesson, our very simple economy consists of just two major sectors: households and businesses. Most modern economies will also have a government sector, which can be a major player in the economy, and the foreign sector, which comprises transactions with the rest of the world.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

In such an economy, the activities center primarily around the goods and services market, the financial markets, and the factors of production market.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
