Aggregate Demand Curve: The Basics<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

First, let’s understand the aggregate demand curve<\/b>, which shows the relationship between the overall price level of goods and services and the real output (real GDP) of an economy. As you may recall, real GDP is also equal to the real income of an economy.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

We’ll need two important curves to derive the aggregate demand curve: the IS curve<\/b> and the LM curve<\/b>. Fortunately, you do not need to know how these two curves are derived for the exam.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
