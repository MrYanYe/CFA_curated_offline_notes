Timberland and Farmland<\/a><\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Step into the green expanse of timberland and farmland as potential alternative investments. Get to know how their income characteristics differ and how returns are influenced by various factors including land and commodity prices. Also, get a reality check on the risks associated with these investments, encompassing low liquidity, high fixed costs, and the vagaries of weather.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Forms of Commodity Investments<\/a><\/h2>\n\n\n\n
