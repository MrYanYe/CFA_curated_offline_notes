Understanding Share-Based Compensation<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Unlike straightforward cash payments, share-based compensation involves rewarding employees with equity in the company. This method doesn’t result in immediate cash outflow but is recognized as an expense, reducing earnings. The fair value estimation of these shares or options can be challenging, especially when they’re not publicly traded or come with specific contingencies.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
