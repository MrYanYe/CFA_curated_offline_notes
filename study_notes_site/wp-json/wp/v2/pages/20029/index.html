What is Own-Price Elasticity of Demand?<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Imagine you’re in the market for some apples. When the price is low, you probably want to buy more. When the price is high, you might want to buy less. If everyone behaves like you, we can plot a downward-sloping demand curve of price against the quantity demanded.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
