Corporate Credit Analysis Essentials | CFA Level I Fixed Income<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

This lesson is dedicated to uncovering the critical aspects that influence a corporation’s credit quality, from its business model to the quantitative metrics that signal its financial health.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Qualitative Factors Influencing Creditworthiness<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Understanding a company’s creditworthiness involves a deep dive into various qualitative factors:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Business Model:<\/b> A stable and predictable business model is pivotal. It includes an analysis of demand for the company’s products or services, expected revenue, and profit margins.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Business Risk:<\/b> This aspect covers the potential deviations in demand, revenue, or margins due to issuer-specific, industry-specific, or external changes.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Competition Analysis:<\/b> Employing Porter\u2019s five forces framework helps assess the competitive environment and its impact on the company.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Corporate Governance:<\/b> Governance policies towards debtholders play a crucial role, focusing on management’s historical actions and policies regarding debt management.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
