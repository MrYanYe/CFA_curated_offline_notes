Auditor’s Report and Other Information Sources | CFA Level I FSA<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

In this lesson, we’ll explore the auditor’s report and other sources of information, like interim reports, proxy statements, press releases, and earnings guidance.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Auditor’s Report<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

An audit<\/b> is an independent review of a firm’s financial statements by an independent certified public accountant. The objective of an audit is to provide an opinion on the fairness and reliability of the financial statements. The standard auditor’s opinion contains three key elements:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • The financial statements are prepared by the management and are its responsibility.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Generally accepted auditing standards were followed, providing reasonable assurance that the financial statements contain no material errors.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • An opinion on whether the statements were prepared in accordance with accepted accounting principles.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

    There are different types of auditor’s opinions:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

      \n
    • Unqualified Opinion:<\/b> Indicates that the auditor believes the statements are free from material omissions and errors.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Qualified Opinion:<\/b> Issued if there are exceptions to the accounting principles, explained in the audit report.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Adverse Opinion:<\/b> Issued if the statements are not presented fairly or are materially nonconforming with accounting standards.<\/li>\n\n\n\n
    • Disclaimer of Opinion:<\/b> Issued if the auditor is unable to express an opinion for any reason.<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n
