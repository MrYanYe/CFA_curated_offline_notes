Investing in Non-Domestic Equity Securities | CFA Level I Equity Investments<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Let’s dive into the world of non-domestic equity securities and explore some of their peculiarities.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Market Integration and Barriers<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

When capital flows freely across borders, markets are said to be integrated<\/b>. Technological innovations and the growth of electronic exchanges have accelerated the integration and growth of global financial markets. However, barriers to global capital flows still exist. Reasons for these barriers include:<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    \n
  • Limiting foreign control on domestic companies<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Reducing volatility of capital flows into and out of domestic equity markets<\/li>\n\n\n\n
  • Requiring domestic ownership in foreign companies<\/li>\n<\/ul>\n\n\n\n

    Studies have shown that reducing barriers improves equity market performance, and companies are increasingly turning to foreign investors for capital.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

    Investing in Non-Domestic Equities: Direct and Indirect Methods<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

    Investing in non-domestic equities can be done directly by buying and selling securities in foreign markets or indirectly through various instruments.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
