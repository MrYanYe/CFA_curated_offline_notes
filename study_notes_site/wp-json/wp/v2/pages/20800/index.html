Understanding Credit Yields and Spreads | CFA Level I Fixed Income<\/h1>\n\n\n\n

Today, we’ll learn about credit yields and spreads, particularly for corporate debt.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

Credit Yields and Spreads<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Recall<\/b> that bonds with credit risk trade at higher yields than risk-free bonds. The difference in yield between a credit-risky bond and a credit-risk-free bond of similar maturity is called its yield spread<\/b>.<\/p>\n\n\n\n

The size of the spread reflects the creditworthiness of the issuer and the liquidity of the market for its bonds. A decrease in creditworthiness or a decrease in liquidity will result in an increase in spread.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
