Understanding Dividend Discount Models<\/h2>\n\n\n\n

Present value models, also known as discounted cash flow models, estimate the intrinsic value of a stock based on the present value of the future benefits<\/strong> expected to be received. The dividend discount model defines such benefits as the expected future dividends<\/strong> to be distributed to shareholders.<\/p>\n\n\n\n
